Construction Projects.

Project Engineer at Ferrocrete Builders, a self-performing concrete subcontractor in Los Angeles, supporting parking and mid-rise construction projects from preconstruction through structure. I prepare concrete estimates and quantity takeoffs, manage RFIs, submittals and change orders, coordinate structural and architectural drawings, develop pour sequences and schedules, and reconcile quantities and costs against field and vendor records. I use technology to make this work faster and more accurate: Bluebeam overlays and AI-assisted drawing review to catch conflicts early, automated invoice and daily-log tracking, and a labor productivity benchmark.

Role & responsibilities.

Project management support

Kept pour dates and production current in the Excel CY tracker for A Street and Bungalows, fed from Buildertrend daily logs and supplier tickets, and built the 19-project productivity and cost dataset. Updated Outbuild schedules from field data and turned 23 schedules into real production rates for planning. Built 3D pour-sequence and shotcrete planning tools so the field, pump and ready-mix supplier worked from one plan. Supported the Project Director and Project Executive.

Document control

Coordinated 212 RFIs and 169 submittals across 8 projects through Autodesk Forma and GC platforms such as Procore. Wrote and filed 53 RFIs and 35 submittals directly, and kept a log for each project. Tracked design revisions with 17 Bluebeam overlays across 6 projects so changes carried through to takeoffs, RFIs and pricing. Wrote daily field reports.

Cost and budget support

Caught $49,631 in duplicate supplier bills and 722 CY of billing errors with an AI-assisted pipeline that reads supplier tickets and matches them to Buildertrend job costing. Built or priced 9 change orders ($112,086), each with Bluebeam takeoff backup tied to sheets and details, and created the firm's COR template. Audited 147 pump and finishing invoices against pour logs. Benchmarked 580,294 labor hours across 19 projects to support estimating.

Field coordination

Linked the office and the field: set up Bluebeam markup sheets for the foreman to record pours by sector, turned field conditions into RFIs, and coordinated layout and sequencing with GCs including JPI, Rockwood, Westport, RAAM and Mill Creek. Tracked the SDG&E inspection hold point at A Street.

Project closeout

Helped draft warranty letters and closeout document packages for completed projects, including Home Depot San Diego, Golden Hill Apartments, Modera Melrose and Brynn. Closed out final invoice, quantity and production records on Melodia, Walnut and Tampa for the company benchmark.

Contracts and scope

Read GC subcontracts, exhibits and scopes of work on 7 projects to confirm what was in and out of Ferrocrete's scope, so out-of-scope work could be priced as change orders.
