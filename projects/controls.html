PROJECT STORY

Make performance legible.

01

The baseline

The report presents a 224-day construction plan with preparation, construction blocks A–C, and final phases. Its stated budget is approximately $1.21 million; these figures form the baseline for the planning study.

02

The process

WBS structuring, activity sequencing, resource and cost loading, and baseline comparisons connect the Gantt programme to time-phased labor, materials, and equipment.

03

Reporting

The archive includes workload analysis, resource assignments, monthly cost reports, cumulative labor curves, a risk register, earned-value dashboards, and schedule-check outputs.

04

What the analysis surfaces

The analysis examines resource over-allocation, early cost overruns, and performance deviations in its examples. These provide a basis for identifying where planning and corrective action are needed; no unsupported savings or delivery improvement is claimed.
