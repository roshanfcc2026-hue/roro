PROJECT STORY

A dialogue with the valley.

01

The context

The site sits along an elevated cliff edge, with panoramic valley views and sunset-facing orientations.

02

The architectural response

Guest spaces and hospitality interiors establish a visual relationship with the surrounding landscape. The presented views emphasize timber, woven textures, planting, and open connections to the valley.

03

Process & documentation

Assisted the principal architect on a two-acre boutique resort, developing architectural drawings, FF&E layouts, and interior elevations for guest rooms, restaurant areas, and public spaces.

04

Delivery approach

The design-bid-build approach connects architectural planning with the detailing of guest rooms and shared hospitality spaces.
