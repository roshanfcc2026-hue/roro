PROJECT STORY

Clinical clarity. Human warmth.

01

The problem

Bring clinical services, an eyewear retail zone, circulation, and a children's play area into one coherent healthcare environment.

02

The design response

Clear zoning and circulation organize the patient journey. A restrained, warm material palette supports an approachable setting while maintaining the clarity required by clinical spaces.

03

My role

Led design development from concept through pre-construction coordination, working with the principal architect, clients, and consultants. Developed presentation sets and coordinated design iterations and permit support.

04

Technical & project controls

Coordinated medical-equipment zoning with architectural, MEP, and HVAC requirements. Prepared BOQs, bid packages, and cost analyses; managed 15+ vendors and suppliers and tracked RFIs, submittals, and QA/QC documentation.

05

Delivery & outcome

Design-bid-build delivery brings clinical areas, reception, retail displays, and a warm material palette together in a functional, patient-focused environment.

06

Context

Professional healthcare and commercial-interior work in Yelahanka, Bangalore.
