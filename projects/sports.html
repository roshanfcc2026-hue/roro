PROJECT STORY

A spine for collective activity.

01

The problem

The thesis addresses a need for sporting infrastructure and public access to facilities in Bangalore, with indoor sports, an aquatic complex, training, and supporting uses.

02

The concept

The human spine becomes a planning analogy: a connecting circulation system links distinct facilities into one composition. Public and service areas sit closer to access roads; curved pathways and organic massing respond to the landscape.

03

Technical development

The design encompasses a 28-acre site, an indoor stadium and training center, a swimming complex, and an administration block. Plans, column layouts, sections, and an exploded structural diagram explain the spatial and structural organization.

04

The outcome

A thesis proposal developed through site analysis, concept sketches, massing studies, plans, sections, and visualizations. Environmental thinking is expressed through landscape integration and orientation; measured performance and built completion are not claimed.
