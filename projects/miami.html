PROJECT STORY

Plan the complexity.

01

My contribution

The stated role covers site logistics coordination, quantity takeoffs, cost estimation, and construction scheduling across the pre-construction and planning phases.

02

Site strategy

The logistics plan places a luffing-jib tower crane, two material-storage zones, temporary worker facilities, and clearly identified entry and exit points within a constrained development site.

03

Cost & time

The study proposes a $147,387,115 project estimate, 714-day programme, and design-bid-build strategy. These are proposed study parameters, not the value or duration of a completed commission.

04

The deliverables

Architectural plans, site logistics, staffing and cost sheets, and schedule extracts connect the tower proposal to a delivery strategy.
